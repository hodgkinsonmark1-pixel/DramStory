## How DramStory makes money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you book through one, we may
receive a commission from the company you book with. **You pay exactly the same
price** — the commission comes out of their margin, not your pocket.

That is currently the only way this site earns anything.

## What this does not affect

This matters more than the disclosure itself, so it is worth being plain.

- **We are not paid by any distillery**, and no distillery pays to appear, to
  rank higher, or to be recommended. DramStory Ltd is not affiliated with any
  distillery unless we say so explicitly.
- **Tour times and prices come from each distillery's own website**, never from
  a third-party aggregator, and never from anyone who pays us.
- **Nobody buys their way into an itinerary.** Which distilleries appear in a
  day, and in what order, is decided by geography, opening hours and what makes
  a good day out.
- **We recommend places we have no arrangement with** — pubs, beaches, walks,
  restaurants, ferries — and always will.

If a commercial arrangement ever changed what we recommend, the site would stop
being worth using. That is a commercial argument as much as an ethical one.

## Your data

Clicking an affiliate link takes you to that company's own website. What they
collect there is governed by their privacy policy, not ours.

The link carries a code identifying DramStory as the referrer. That code
identifies **us**, not you — we do not learn who you are from it, and we
receive no personal data back. We are told only that a booking happened.

See our Privacy Policy for what we do and do not collect.
