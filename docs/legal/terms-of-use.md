## 4 · Accuracy, and its limits

We take accuracy seriously. Tour times and prices come from each distillery's
own website, are recorded with the date we checked, and are re-checked
regularly.

**But things change, often at short notice**, and a page here can be out of
date without us knowing. Opening hours change seasonally. Tours sell out.
Distilleries close for maintenance. Ferries do not sail in weather.

**Always confirm directly with the venue before you travel or spend money.**

rather than quoting it.
