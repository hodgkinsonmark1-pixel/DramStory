

## 31 Aug 2026 — Ardnahoe's tagline

WAS: "Islay's newest distillery — and its highest."
NOW: "Islay's only worm tubs — copper coils, cooled the old way."

The old line had been false since Laggan Bay began producing in April
2026, and its second half became redundant when the homepage badge
changed to "Highest Distillery" the same day.

Sourced entirely from ardnahoedistillery.com's own Our Process page:
"Ardnahoe Distillery is unique on Islay and one of only a handful in
Scotland still to employ the traditional style of worm tub condenser",
and "Large copper coils submerged in tanks or 'tubs' of cold water".

Second-pass review rejected the first draft ("Height of the Hollow — and
the only worm tubs on Islay"). Two findings worth keeping:
  - "Height" sat two lines under a "Highest Distillery" badge and read as
    a stutter, implying Ardnahoe MEANS highest. Same-card adjacency is a
    thing to check, not just the sentence on its own.
  - "worm tubs" alone is production-floor jargon. The persona for this
    field is a visitor deciding whether to spend two hours of a trip
    driving there, and the tagline renders on map popups and grid cards
    with no surrounding copy to explain it. Hence "copper coils, cooled
    the old way" - the term made concrete rather than assumed.

58 characters, so it clears the 60-char truncateSummary cap on
/areas/[slug] and renders whole in all six places the tagline appears.
